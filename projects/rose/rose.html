A futuristic 3D fashion experience for Mexico Fashion Week. Designers create base designs through the app, from which the ROSE algorithm generates a collection that walks down a virtual runway.

How might fashion designers collaborate with an AI?

In this future scenario, fashion collections are generated via algorithmic selection. Designers feed quantitative preferences into software, which interpolates those choices into a fashion collection.

In the artistic spirit of the project, the creative direction called for abstract, geometric designs that had recognizable clothing qualities but did not try to be real clothing.

Design Approach

I organized the experience into a tool for fashion designers with 4 steps.

01CutManipulate sliders that control the shape of the garments.

02EmbellishPlace geometric shapes across the surface of the garments.

03MaterialsChoose materials and upload patterns.

04GenerateSelect looks generated by the ROSE algorithm.

The ROSE Algorithm

I wrote ROSE as a basic genetic algorithm. At each generation it creates 18 looks from the selected features and the two looks chosen in the previous generation.

The user progresses through 5 generations, and the looks become gradually more extreme.

While ROSE was purely conceptual, one has to wonder: as product algorithms become more commonplace and robust, what becomes of the role of the designer? Could a computer design things better matched to individual tastes and shopping habits?
