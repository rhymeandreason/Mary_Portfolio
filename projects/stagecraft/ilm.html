UI/UX Design

Popularized by The Mandalorian — real-time, photo-real virtual sets indistinguishable from their physical counterparts.

Domain Context

During shoots, crew spend significant time physically inside the LED volume. The iPad app lets DPs and VFX supervisors move and control elements in real time while standing on set.

Primary features include moving white cards, flags, and bounce cards, and adjusting light size, intensity, and softness. Color correction, garbage mattes, and exposure blend can all be controlled live. Top-tier users demand great precision and fine-tuning.

I worked with two outstanding R&D engineers to create the first design system for their iPad controller app — improving usability and building a scalable foundation for a growing set of features.
